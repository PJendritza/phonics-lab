# Audio: human German targets and complete-sentence instructions

76 complete human German target recordings, 11 isolated Kalulu phonics feedback clips, 22 full-sentence Piper instructions, 26 optional sentence/phrase readings (including six legacy sentence clips) and one
quiet nonverbal success chime. Three old synthetic a/i/m clips are adult-comparison
only. No new synthetic isolated phoneme or letter-name substitute; no consonant
or vowel was cropped from a word. The user liked the human targets on their phone and approved the Thorsten High sample direction, then requested 1.5 with a 0.3-second sentence pause. This is not blanket phonetic approval of every regenerated instruction.

app/audio/manifest.json records individual speakers, source/license versions,
original/output SHA256, processing and pending review. docs/audio-sources.json
preserves original Commons metadata; docs/reading-audio-sources.json pins the 35 new word sources and their authors/licenses/checksums. In-app About and Töne prüfen expose individual credits.

## Phonetic scope

Isolated a/i/o/e/u retain existing human recordings. m is the complete natural hum
from hm. Short vowels are heard in whole am/im/um/an/in, never cut from words.
ma/mi use whole recorded Ma and mi as teaching units. Whole nouns retain stress
and vowel variation rather than synthesized one-letter/one-sound assumptions.

Task cues use whole human words for first/last-sound identification: f/n/l/sch,
b/d/g/h initially, and f/s/sch/t/k finally. These task cues are context exercises. Wrong-letter feedback now uses the isolated clips detailed in docs/PHONEMES.md rather than context words. Initial b/d/g avoid final devoicing; final s is /s/,
not German initial voiced /z/. sch and ll are single blocks in relevant tasks.
No guessed recording-specific IPA is certified. Mixed speakers remain audible;
purpose-recording with one native speaker is a future quality improvement.

In v4, 21 new complete-word recordings were obtained with individual Commons metadata and
pinned source hashes. Five then-optional candidates (das/bis/mit/gut/fit) hit HTTP 429
after bounded retries and were omitted in that release. Release 7 adds a pinned full recording for das; bis/mit/gut/fit remain absent. Do not keep
retrying absent sources or manufacture isolated phonemes to fill gaps.

## Reproduction

Human processing: quiet margins shortened retaining 120/160 ms, -20 LUFS/-3 dB
true-peak target, 8 ms leading fade, 120 ms trailing pad; mono 44.1 kHz 80 kbps MP3.
No pitch/time manipulation, phoneme extraction or stitched speech. Ordinary builds
use committed MP3s and need no synthesis engine/model.

Optional regeneration requires ffmpeg and external piper-tts==1.4.2:

```sh
/workspace/kalulu-tools/tts/bin/python scripts/prepare_audio.py
# Sentence instructions only, preserving processed human clips/effect:
/workspace/kalulu-tools/tts/bin/python scripts/prepare_audio.py --instructions-only
python3 scripts/make_feedback.py
python3 scripts/audit_audio.py
```

Pass --cache/--model for other external locations. Human sources are read from the
manifest and downloaded via verified TLS with pinned hashes. The cache used for
new original files is /workspace/phonics-audio-v4; earlier originals are under
/workspace/kalulu-browser-audio. A full regeneration can download missing originals.
Instructions live in scripts/instructions.json. prepare_audio.py preserves effects;
make_feedback.py independently generates the own 240 ms two-note sine chime. No
spoken success/retry/navigation clips remain in the active manifest. Different
ffmpeg/Piper versions can change bytes; review hashes and rerun tests after changes.

## Review and verification

Töne prüfen filters letters, syllables, words, instructions and effects. Ratings/
notes are local and export with clip hashes, never silently approving source audio.
Use phone speakers/headphones to assess starts/ends, length, stress and context
instructions. Native-speaker/educator review remains pending for added material.

Audit decodes all 62 current clips, checks nonzero PCM, peak headroom and quiet last
50 ms. Browser tests decode all 65 MP3s including comparisons and exercise actual
playback, retry/cancellation and optional feedback failure. Measurements do not
prove pronunciation, instruction comprehensibility or educational efficacy.

## Isolated wrong-letter feedback (v6)

Eleven licensed Kalulu phonics extracts provide f/n/l/s/sch/b/d/g/t/k/h; existing human vowel/m clips and l for ll complete the feedback map. See [source/processing details](PHONEMES.md). No original German word was cropped, replaced or synthesized. These recordings are phonetic transfers from French/Portuguese teaching assets, not certified native-German speech. Their source segments and pending review are visible in the manifest/review page. In a letter task, only the selected phone plays; in a word-reading task, the selected word still plays.

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare_phonemes.py
```

This optional regeneration uses ffmpeg and pinned upstream URLs/hashes, with originals cached outside source. prepare_audio.py preserves these phoneme entries when regenerating sentence guidance. The original 41 German targets and nine approved instruction MP3s remain byte-identical in this update.

## Expanded reading vocabulary (v7)

Thirty-five additional whole-word human recordings bring the word bank to 58
word/numeral/function-word entries. Their exact sources, authors, license versions
and hashes are in reading-audio-sources.json and the active manifest. No new
phoneme synthesis, cropped consonants or stitched word speech. New word models
precede independent reading lessons.

Twelve new full-sentence instructions and fourteen optional sentence readings use
the unchanged Thorsten High model/settings. Independent word/sentence-to-picture
questions never autoplay their answer. Explicit read-aloud and syllable help count
as assistance, preserved in saved progress. Sentence support remains separately
auditionable in Töne prüfen → Sätze. All old 62 active MP3 files stay byte-identical.

```sh
/workspace/kalulu-tools/tts/bin/python scripts/prepare_reading_audio.py
```

The script verifies pinned source and model/config hashes, reuses external caches
and does not replace the existing word/phoneme/guide assets. Base prepare_audio.py
now retains the reading-specific TTS entries when regenerating foundation guides.
Wikimedia temporarily rate-limited discovery; downloads were paused according to
the server retry interval, then resumed at a reduced pace. Full technical and live
verification outcomes belong in PROGRESS.md. No native/educator listening approval
is claimed. Review especially weak syllables, voiced s, vowel length, ie/ei and
final b in Laub/Sieb; a dedicated final-devoicing lesson remains future work.

## v9 clarity and short-text help

All 76 human targets and all 11 isolated phone files remain unchanged. Three
previous instructions are revised: soft_words now uses concrete Tomate/Banane
examples without a bare E/I label; sentence_words introduces the earlier small
words; sentence_where refers to small relation words as well as object/place.
The new phrase_read instruction and twelve optional text readings are added
(four short phrases plus eight am sentences). Exact settings remain Thorsten High
length_scale 1.5 and an added 0.3 s sentence pause. Original im sentences and six
legacy noun-only sentence recordings remain for valid pending-data resume.

Wrong pictures and invented written forms receive no spoken label or invented
pronunciation. Real written choices with an existing human model retain that
feedback. After two mistakes only the original target is modelled. No isolated
phoneme synthesis or changed human word pronunciation is claimed.
