# Accurate word recognition and meaningful comprehension (v9)

The latest user request explicitly authorises closer written distractors plus
the previously planned instruction, picture-feedback, artwork and curriculum
changes. This supersedes the earlier planning-only hold for those changes.

## Close written alternatives

Only whole-word auditory recognition receives invented written forms. Basic
letter/syllable tasks, block building and picture answers retain their own logic.
Success on phonemes alone does not make an unseen word difficult immediately.
Word-specific/stage word history controls the extra challenge: an intermediate
step adds one close alternative, and strong performance makes all alternatives
start alike with a one-letter internal/final difference. Recent errors lower the
next word question's challenge. Choice count still adapts separately.

The finite authored forms in word-choices.mjs preserve the first letter, length
and taught alphabet. They keep sch/ei/au/ie/ee/oo and consonant doubling intact
where relevant, rather than introducing silent-letter or spelling-only traps.
See/Tee keep ordinary recorded-word alternatives: changing one noninitial letter
in those three-letter words cannot preserve both ee and their length.
The sieben set changes the medial consonant, avoiding n/m ambiguity caused by
normal pronunciation assimilation. Forms can coincide with rare words or names;
this is not a guaranteed dictionary/nonword classification or a new word list.

An available real same-stage word is preferred when its spelling matches a form
(for example Oma/Omi and Hase/Hose). Other forms are silent comparison options,
not speaking models or learning targets. No invented audio or phoneme synthesis,
skill keys, mastery, or spaced-review entries are created for them. Wrong real
written words with existing recordings keep their own feedback; invented forms
use a quiet visual retry. Two errors still teach the original correct model and
restore the original choices without extra points or false first-try mastery.
Exact chosen alternatives survive pause, navigation, reload and backup.

## Picture feedback and clearer instructions

Wrong word-to-picture and sentence/phrase-to-picture choices no longer read the
wrong label or another sentence. Choices remain still and the written prompt
remains visible. Original-target speech is available explicitly and in the
existing two-error teaching step. Isolated-letter feedback is unchanged.

The vague weak-e explanation was replaced by “Hör auf das Ende von Tomate und
Banane. Finde genau das Wort, das du hörst.” The instruction no longer depends
on a synthetic bare E/I letter name. All human word/phoneme recordings remain
unchanged; this is not a claim that a native pronunciation defect was diagnosed.
Schwa/vowel-length explanations belong in adult documentation. New full utterances
use the approved free local Thorsten High settings (1.5 / extra 0.3-second pause).

## Sequence and meaningful choices

The first 14 levels retain their numbers/IDs. Common sentence words move from
Level 30 to Level 15, using the same ID/skills and complete human models. ein/eine
receive explicit whole-word exposure and optional ei highlighting during teaching;
later ei word lessons provide further transfer. It is not pure shape memorisation.

Level 16 continues independent familiar-word reading. New Level 17 introduces
short phrases: Ball im Bus, Ball am Bus, Hut im Bus, Hut am Bus. Every token has
appeared earlier. These are word groups, not falsely labelled full sentences.
At least three choices contain same-subject and same-relation alternatives, so
recognising one word cannot uniquely identify the answer.

Level 32 replaces the old constant-stem/noun-only task with full im/am sentences
using two subjects and one place. Level 33 expands to four subjects, two places
and both relations. At least four choices contrast subject, relation and location
one dimension at a time. Each keyword individually matches multiple alternatives.
These minima are necessary for the comprehension task, with one-choice teaching
available after errors. Static, familiar pictures support the meaning contrasts.
This is still controlled sentence comprehension, not stories or an efficacy claim.

## Migration

Storage stays version 3, with curriculumVersion 9. All stable stage IDs, scores,
flowers, old skill history, paused tasks and goal totals are preserved. Numbered
positions after 14 change because words move earlier and a phrase level is added.
Existing in-progress cursors follow their stage IDs; newly earlier material is
available in the map without discarding their current work.

Old s_* sentences/recordings are retained as legacyTargets for exact saved task
or old review resume. Subsequent new questions use the active meaning contrasts.
Old noun-only/inside-only pass flags are reassessed for the expanded activities;
old records and rewards are not deleted or treated as new comprehension evidence.
Previously completed courses continue with the new phrases. Forward continuation
skips already-passed intervening material, then reaches the new comprehension
targets, avoiding a forced replay of the old course. Explicit review still returns
to the latest saved forward cursor. v6 completion begins at the newly earlier
sentence-word introduction. No backwards auto-review of weak old lessons is added.
